# Messaging and Paid Communications

Private messaging, paid entry/messages, PPV attachments, mass messaging, notifications and abuse controls.

# 11. Messaging, Paid DMs and PPV Messages

Phase 6 uses a normalized creator/viewer conversation with participant read state. Eligibility and pricing are resolved server-side; a locked attachment references the existing private media asset and is authorised only after a settled message-unlock record. Message unlocks reuse `PaymentAttempt` and the immutable ledger rather than creating a wallet or upload pipeline. Campaign recipients are snapshotted at execution time and unique per campaign to make worker replay safe.


## 11.1 Messaging permissions

- Anyone can message
- Followers only
- Subscribers only
- Previous customers
- Nobody / disabled
- Optional paid entry to message


## 11.2 Pay-per-message

Creators can define a price required for a non-exempt viewer to initiate/send a message. Example: subscribers free, non-subscribers €2 per message or per conversation entry according to selected model.


## 11.3 PPV message attachments

- Free text with locked attachment.
- Paid text/message where product policy permits.
- Locked photo, gallery or video entitlement purchased inside the conversation.
- Price shown before purchase; attachment remains inaccessible until entitlement succeeds.


## 11.4 Mass messaging

- All followers
- All subscribers
- Expired subscribers
- Previous purchasers
- Top spenders / creator-defined segment
- Custom list

Mass messaging requires platform rate limits, opt-out/preferences, anti-spam controls and moderation visibility. Creators/groups with delegated permission may attach PPV media or offers.

# 25. Referral and Affiliate Systems


## 24.1 Referral types

- Fan -> Fan: credits/reward to referrer and optionally referred user.
- Creator -> Creator: e.g. percentage of platform revenue generated by referred creator for a defined period.
- Agency referral: separate business terms.
- Campaign-specific referral incentives.

Creator referral rewards should preferably be funded from the platform commission rather than silently reducing the referred creator's agreed earnings unless terms explicitly say otherwise.


## 24.2 Referral tracking

- Referral code/link
- Click and signup attribution
- Verification conversion
- Qualified/active creator conversion
- Revenue generated
- Commission earned
- Attribution window
- Self-referral and fraud controls
- Reversal policy for refunded/charged-back activity


## 24.3 Affiliate programme

Affiliate functionality should be separate from simple referral incentives and support tracking links, campaign IDs, sub IDs, creatives, conversion reports, attribution windows, payouts and fraud controls.
